About me

I care less about building a pretty chart and more about the decision it changes.

I'm a Business Data Analyst with ~3.5 years of experience in SaaS and service-driven businesses. I work in the gap between "we have a lot of data" and "we know what to do next."

My toolkit is SQL, Power BI, Python and Excel. My best work removes busywork: I've automated reporting that cut manual effort by ~30%, built 10+ leadership dashboards, and set up validation and reconciliation that improved reporting accuracy by 20%.

I hold an MS in Business Analytics from Trine University. I'm looking for a senior analytics role where I can push data-informed growth and operational excellence.
